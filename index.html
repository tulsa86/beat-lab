Your first beat, in a minute.

  1. Press Play. Your starter beat is already waiting. Headphones help you hear the bass.
  2. Make it yours. Tap drum squares to add or remove hits. Each group of four is one beat.
  3. Add some notes. Pick a bass or melody note, then tap steps to place it. Tap the same note again to erase it. All notes follow your selected key and scale.
  4. Try something new. Change the tempo, move the volume sliders, or hit Surprise me for a new editable loop.
  5. Keep your idea. Give it a name and Save project. Load brings back your saved loops on this browser.

On iPhone, keep this page open while playing. Switching apps or taking a call pauses the beat; tap Play when you return. If you cannot hear it, check your device volume, silent mode, and audio output.
